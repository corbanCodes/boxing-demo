Ratings Principles

How fighters are rated

The purpose of the UCA ratings is to identify and track the nation’s top boxers in each weight class and determine eligible contenders to participate in title elimination bouts, interim title bouts and championship title bouts.

Only the top 10 of each weight class are listed in the rankings.

Rating factors

  • Win/loss record, level of competition and activity
  • Athletic ability, weight class and experience
  • Defeating a rated boxer or a former champion
  • Winning important amateur tournaments — Golden Gloves, Police Olympics
  • A successful career in other combat sports
  • Special circumstance, or by recommendation

Women’s division note

Due to the few Public Safety women presently boxing, full-time security officers, EMTs, paramedics and tribal police/security are ranked and have the opportunity to fight for a UCA Championship Title.
