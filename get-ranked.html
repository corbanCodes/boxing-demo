Are You Ranked?

Sign Up to Compete

Full-time or retired Law Enforcement, Corrections, Fire, non-sworn department employees or active U.S. Military, 21 and over? You are eligible to compete, be ranked, and fight for a UCA Championship Title. Fill this out and a committee member will be in touch.

Prefer to talk it through? Call Tom Gaffney at 916-612-6865 or email uca500@yahoo.com.

Who can compete

  • 21 years of age or older
  • Full-time active or retired Law Enforcement, Correctional Officer, Firefighter, non-sworn department employee, or active U.S. Military
  • Women’s division also open to full-time security officers, EMTs, paramedics and tribal police/security
  • No prior professional boxing or professional individual combat sport experience

What happens next

  • A committee member reviews your details
  • We match you by age, weight, height, experience and record
  • You’ll need a physician-signed physical within 6 months of the event
  • Win, and you enter the rankings — then start the climb to a title
