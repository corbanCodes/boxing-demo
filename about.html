Who We Are

About the UCA

A ranking, sanctioning and promotional organization built solely for Public Safety boxing — uniting peace officers, firefighters and military personnel with fighter safety as the top priority.

The United Combat Association is a ranking organization for male and female Law Enforcement, Military and Firefighters who compete in amateur boxing.

A ranking system establishes the #1 and #2 ranked fighters in each division and gives them the opportunity to compete for the United Combat Association Championship title. Through boxing, the UCA hopes to build camaraderie between all Public Safety agencies and develop friendships, a sense of community pride, honor, loyalty and good faith throughout the country.

The focus is to increase participation and build solidarity among diverse agencies, organizations and departments. The UCA’s goal is to spread public safety boxing worldwide and give aspiring boxers — who put their lives on the line every day — the chance to make a name for themselves by publicly recognizing their talent in the ring.

The UCA is dedicated to bringing fans the highest level of Public Safety boxing year-round. Our commitment is to discover incredible new talent from all agencies, male and female, in all weight classes. Once the top-rated boxers are established, they compete for the most prestigious award in Public Safety boxing: the UCA Championship Title Belt.
