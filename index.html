Who We Are

One ring.
Every badge.

The UCA is a ranking, sanctioning and promotional organization built solely for Public Safety boxing. We unite peace officers, firefighters and military personnel in competitive, safety-first boxing — building camaraderie and solidarity across every agency and department.

Our goal is to spread public safety boxing worldwide and give aspiring boxers the chance to make a name for themselves. Once the top-rated fighters are established, they compete for the most prestigious prize in the sport: the UCA Championship Title Belt.
