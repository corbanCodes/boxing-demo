Who can compete

  • 21 years of age or older
  • Full-time active or retired Law Enforcement, Correctional Officer, Firefighter, non-sworn department employee, or active U.S. Military
  • Women’s division also open to full-time security officers, EMTs, paramedics and tribal police
  • No prior professional boxing or combat-sport experience

What happens next

  • A committee member reviews your details
  • We match you by age, weight, height, experience and record
  • You’ll need a physician-signed physical within 6 months
  • Win, and you enter the rankings
